Reflection

Ticket 101 performed well because Inventory used the actual zero stock and did not promise a shipment while invoice 501 was open. Ticket 102 correctly prepared rent without silently paying. Ticket 103 surfaced the shortage and preserved a margin decision for Accounting.

The Actual handoffs matched Expected: Boss chose a specialist first, and a second specialist was only needed for the follow-up. A single agent would be simpler for a small lookup like ticket 102 because it has less coordination overhead, but specialist handoffs are clearer for inventory, money, and customer communication together.

Three solvable new problems are a mug restock, a cap availability question, and a routine rent reminder. Three not solvable with these tools are accepting a credit-card payment (needs a payment processor), shipping a package (needs shipping/order tools), and handling a damaged-item photo (needs image intake and returns tools). I would add Payment, Fulfillment, and Returns agents plus those tools.
